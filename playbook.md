## 2. Account setup and voice

- **Bio (every platform):** `GTA 6 news, tips and the best clips, fast. Fan page, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.` Never write "official".
- **Profile picture:** a simple mark that reads at small size, such as a letter mark, a neon palm or a radar/map pin. Never use Rockstar's logo or key art.
- **Banner:** a clean neon or sunset look with the page name and one line on what we post. Make it ourselves.
- **Pinned post:** a "what this page is" post at first, then the best performer.
- **Voice:** hype but clean. Short, punchy headlines in sentence case. One emoji max, used as an accent (🌴 ⏳ 👀 🎥). Write "GTA 6", the most-searched form. Never clickbait something the post doesn't deliver.
- **AI voiceover style:** 2.5 words per second, so a 15 s clip has about 35 words. Open with the hook and end with "Follow for GTA 6 news, fast." Same voice every time so it becomes recognisable. If a platform asks whether content uses AI, answer honestly.

## 6. Credit and permission rules

1. Public posts only. Skip anything protected, deleted or taken down.
2. Credit every clip on its own line: `🎥 @creator`. For official material: `🎥 @RockstarGames`. For photos: `📸 @creator`.
3. Credit is not permission. When a creator is clearly reachable, ask with a quick DM or reply: "Love this clip, ok if we share it with credit?" Prefer creators who allow sharing.
4. Transform it. Trim, add an on-screen hook, add captions, add our own commentary, AI voiceover or tip text. Don't just re-upload the whole video.
5. If anyone asks us to take something down, take it down the same day with no argument, and log it in [takedowns.md](takedowns.md). Then don't use that creator again without permission.
6. Never post leaks, datamined files, leaked builds, leaked maps or tracklists, pirated copies, "how to play early" tricks or code exploits. If you can't tell whether footage is official or leaked, don't use it.
7. Don't name leak outlets or rumor sources in posts.
8. Rockstar's game footage is their property, and fan pages operate on their tolerance. Keep it respectful, never sell their content, never imply we're Rockstar.

## 7. Clipping workflow

**Tools**

- **Long videos (streams, long YouTube videos):** an AI clipping tool such as OpusClip or Klap to find highlights and reframe to vertical. Check each tool's current free-plan limits and spend the free minutes on the longest, best sources.
- **Short clips and edits:** CapCut (trim, crop, captions, text-to-speech for the AI voice). ffmpeg works too.
- Always review AI picks. AI clippers miss context and can pick a spoiler or a bad moment.

**Make it ours (every clip)**

1. **Hook in the first second:** on-screen text saying why to keep watching ("THE HIDDEN CAR NOBODY FOUND", "IS GTA 6 ON PC?").
2. **Tight trim:** 7 to 30 seconds for most clips. Cut dead air at the start and end on the payoff.
3. **Captions:** burned in, large, centered in the safe area, high contrast. Many people watch muted.
4. **Add value:** AI voiceover with a fact or tip, a label, a then-vs-now split, or a compilation with our own framing. Plain reposts get buried.
5. **No spoilers in the hook or thumbnail** for story moments. Label story clips "SPOILER" in the caption.
6. Mark sensitive media where needed. The game is rated M, so skip nudity frames entirely.

**AI voiceover steps (CapCut)**

1. Write the script from [facts.md](facts.md) only (drafts in [idea-bank.md](idea-bank.md)).
2. Text → text-to-speech → pick one voice and keep using it.
3. Duck the game audio under the voice, and keep the captions matching the voice word for word.

**Specs** (check current limits, they change)

| Platform | Aspect ratio | Resolution | Length notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Short wins for clips. 7 to 30 s default. |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 (or 1:1) | 1080×1920 | Up to 3 minutes. |
| X | 16:9 or 9:16 | 1080p | Non-Premium accounts can upload up to 140 seconds. Landscape trailer clips are fine natively. |
| Instagram Reels (later) | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Keep under 3 minutes. 7 to 30 s default. |

Export MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. Prefer 1080p and skip 4K files (big uploads, no gain).

## 9. Uploading and scheduling through Buffer

Buffer can publish to X, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky and more.
Some channels need a newer or paid plan, so ask the owner before changing plans.

1. **Connect channels:** add each account in Buffer as a channel.
2. **Confirm the right channel** every time before scheduling. Check that the name and avatar match our page.
3. **Set times explicitly** in Europe/Warsaw time. Don't trust a default queue you didn't set up.
4. **Upload the video file,** not a link to someone else's post. Automations need a direct, public, permanent HTTPS link to the MP4.
5. **Per-platform tweaks** in the composer: no hashtags on X, a few on TikTok and Shorts, and pick a cover frame.
6. **Fallback:** if the video upload fails, post the caption with a link to the original public post (X shows it as a quote) or skip the slot. Never post the clip without credit.
7. **Breaking official news:** use "Share now" instead of the queue. Speed matters more than the slot.
8. Some features may not auto-publish. Buffer sends a reminder so you can post those manually from the app.
9. After it publishes, log it in [log.md](log.md) and [scoreboard.csv](scoreboard.csv).

## 10. Daily routine (Warsaw time)

- **16:00 Start:** check official sources and overnight news (the US evening happened while you slept). Check the Buffer queue for gaps and duplicates. Read metrics for posts that are 12, 24 or 72 hours old and update the scoreboard.
- **16:00–16:30 Batch:** finalize today's 7 posts from [idea-bank.md](idea-bank.md). Schedule all of them in Buffer, including the late slots 6 and 7.
- **Every 2 to 3 hours while you're up:** quick scan for official news, then the engagement block (section 11).
- **Before bed:** one line in [learning.md](learning.md) (best post, worst post, one lesson) and pick tomorrow's test.

## 11. Engagement routine (2 to 3 times a day, about 15 minutes)

- Reply to comments on our posts in the first hour. Answer questions with real info.
- Reply under official Rockstar posts and big GTA posts with something useful (a verified fact, a quick tip) in the page's voice. No "follow me" spam.
- Thank creators we credited, and tag them where it's natural. They often repost.
- Quote official Rockstar posts with a short take or fact list.
- Save every good question to the idea bank.
- Never argue, never engage with leak drama, never DM people asking for follows.

## 12. Big-drop playbook

Launch day (November 19, 2026) has passed. Reuse this for every big drop: a PC
announcement, a trailer, a patch, DLC or GTA Online news.

**Before (when a date is announced):** add it to [facts.md](facts.md), pre-make an
"OUT NOW" post with official art, pre-write the first HOW TO posts from official facts
only, and clear the Buffer queue around the drop so live posts aren't buried.

**At the drop:**

1. Post on X the moment it's live and confirmed by an official source.
2. Watch official channels and early streams, and clip the first strong moments.
3. Edit fast (hook, trim, captions, AI voice, credit). Aim to post within 15 to 30 minutes.
4. Post to X first because it's fastest, then TikTok and Shorts.
5. Keep posting every 1 to 2 hours for the first 48 hours if you can, going above 7 that day.
6. Turn every repeated question in the comments into a HOW TO post the same day.
7. Never post leaked footage or "play early" tricks. Being first means first after the legit release.

## 13. Learning loop

Log every post in [scoreboard.csv](scoreboard.csv) with these columns: `post_id, date,
time, platform, pillar, format, hook, length_sec, credit, views_12h, views_24h,
views_72h, likes, reposts_or_shares, replies_or_comments, saves_or_bookmarks,
followers_at_read, test_name, notes`.

1. Fill metrics at 12 h, 24 h and 72 h. If a number isn't available, leave it blank. Never guess a number.
2. **One test per day.** Change one variable only (hook style, length, posting time, caption length, still vs clip, official vs creator clip, AI voice vs text-only). Name the test in the log.
3. **No verdict under 24 hours.** Prefer the 72 h read if the post is still moving.
4. **Daily review:** best and worst post, plus one lesson written as a rule ("HOW TO posts with 3 steps beat 5 steps").
5. **Weekly review:** top 3 and bottom 3 posts, median views by pillar, by time slot and by platform. Double down on what's above median, and cut or rework what's been below median for a full week. Pick next week's tests.
6. Write every lesson into [learning.md](learning.md) under Wins, Losses and Current rules.
7. Study 3 to 5 bigger pages in the niche every week: which formats, hooks and times win for them. Copy formats, never wording.

## 14. Safety and accuracy rules

1. **Verify before posting.** Every fact needs an official source (Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, Xbox). Log it with the link in [facts.md](facts.md). Recheck anything older than 48 hours.
2. **No fake news.** Never invent dates, numbers, quotes, features or "insider" claims. Third-party sales estimates stay out unless clearly labeled as estimates.
3. **Rumors stay out.** The owner chose no approvals, so there's nobody to approve one. Unconfirmed reports (e.g. an unannounced PC date) don't get posted.
4. **No leaks, piracy, exploits or "play early" tricks.** Ever.
5. **No AI-generated "screenshots" or "gameplay" passed off as real.** The AI voice is fine; fake footage is not.
6. **Not affiliated:** never imply we're Rockstar. No fake giveaways or "free copy" posts.
7. **Follow each platform's rules** on sensitive media, spam, impersonation, copyright and AI disclosure. Take content down the same day on request.
8. **Ask the owner first** for any sponsorship or paid promo, any plan upgrade or purchase, and any change to the name or bio.
9. **Day counts:** only post numbers you can reproduce. Count calendar days in Europe/Warsaw. November 19, 2026 is Day 1 in Leonida.
10. **Never share passwords, logins or private info** in chat or in these files.

## 17. Growth and money reality

- New pages grow from people who don't follow them yet. Judge posts on impressions and non-follower reach, not follower count.
- Speed on official news + useful tips + good credited clips = trust, and trust compounds.
- The "$10,000 a month" in the original post is a hook, not a plan. Expect no income for the first months. Rockstar's publisher, Take-Two, is known for copyright takedowns.
- **Platform payouts** (checked 2026-11-20 via third-party guides, so confirm in each app's creator settings):
  - **YouTube Partner Program:** 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days. Reports say the bar for new applicants rises from February 2027, possibly to 20M. Reuploads and compilations without real commentary get rejected as "reused/inauthentic content", and so does AI content without human input.
  - **X:** the old revenue sharing closed to new members in August 2026. The replacement, "Original Content Rewards", pays for original content, so reposted clips won't count.
  - **TikTok Creator Rewards:** 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, videos over 1 minute. Poland appears **not** to be eligible right now.
- **What this means for us:** lean on our own written news, our own scripts and our own commentary over official footage, not plain reposts. That's what the payout programs reward.
- **Other income, once there's an audience:** sponsorships and affiliate links. Mark every paid post as an ad, and get the owner's approval first (section 14, rule 8). Never sell the account, which breaks every platform's rules.
