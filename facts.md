## Day count

November 19, 2026 is **Day 1 in Leonida**, counting calendar days in Europe/Warsaw.
Day N = (today's date in Warsaw − 2026-11-19) + 1. So 2026-11-20 is Day 2 and
2026-11-26 is Day 8.
